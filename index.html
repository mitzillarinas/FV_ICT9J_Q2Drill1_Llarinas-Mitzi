🏸 Beginner's Guide to Badminton

📋 Basic Rules
  • Serve underhand below the waist
  • Rally scoring up to 21 points
  • Shuttlecock must land in bounds
  • Only one hit per side to return
🎒 Badminton Equipment
  • Badminton Racket
  • Shuttlecock (Feather/Nylon)
  • Badminton Net
  • Non-marking Court Shoes
🎯 Basic Techniques
  • Forehand and Backhand Grip
  • High and Low Serves
  • Smash and Drop Shots
  • Footwork and Court Coverage
