Badminton Beginner's Guide

Basic Rules

• Serve below the waist line.

• Matches are played to 21 points.

• The shuttlecock must stay in bounds.

• Only 1 hit allowed per side.

Equipment Needed

• Racket

• Shuttlecock

• Badminton Net

• Court Shoes

Key Techniques

• Proper Forehand Grip

• High and Low Serves

• Smash and Drop Shots
